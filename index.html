Reserve My Spot
The problem isn’t a lack of information. It’s the gap between knowing what is possible and knowing how to make it happen for yourself.

That’s why we created a personalised, hands-on workshop that starts with a detailed pre-workshop questionnaire and ends with something far more useful than a notebook full of notes: a practical understanding of how to put your points to work, identify opportunities and plan your next trip more strategically.

From making your holidays more affordable to exploring Business Class flights and exceptional hotel stays, we’ll help you learn how to find the opportunities that fit your goals.

Is it for you?

This Workshop is for you if…

You don’t need to know everything about miles and points before you walk in. You just need to be curious about what your spending could unlock.

PRE-FLIGHT CHECKTICK ALL THAT APPLY

  • You spend ₹20 lakh or more a year.

    Whether you’re a business owner or a working professional, your everyday spending could be doing more for your travel.

  • You enjoy travelling well.

    You appreciate luxury hotels, memorable vacations, comfortable flights and an elevated travel experience, and you’re curious about how to get more of them.

  • You want more from the money you already spend.

    You’re not necessarily looking to spend more on travel. You want to become smarter about earning, transferring and redeeming your rewards.

  • You want a strategy, not another list of credit cards.

    You’re ready to understand how the different pieces fit together and apply that knowledge to your own situation.

The Workshop Day

A full day, in a small room, on what matters most: using your points to travel better.

  1. 01

    Learn the Game

    Understand the rules

    Discover how credit cards, reward currencies, transfer partners, airline miles and hotel loyalty programmes fit together. Learn the complicated world of airline frequent flyer programmes, their idiosyncrasies and nuances, and how everything else fits into their universe.

  2. 02

    Understand How the Pros Play

    Tips, tricks and hacks

    Learn how to approach any booking, how to plan and what to look out for. We’ll share the strategies, shortcuts and hard-earned lessons that help you avoid common mistakes and make better decisions. And we may drop some hacks in there as well!

  3. 03

    Get on the Pitch

    Practice sessions

    This is where theory meets application. Work through your upcoming travel plans, explore redemption options and practise evaluating flights, hotel stays, transfer opportunities and award availability to upgrade your next vacation.

  4. 04

    Review the Match

    Live Q&A and next steps

    We start with a detailed question-and-answer session, and end with the steps you should take once the workshop is over.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the pre-workshop questionnaire?

Before the workshop, we ask you about your cards, spending patterns, existing points, travel preferences and goals. We use it to tailor the day to the people in the room, so the examples and practice sessions match what you actually want to do.

When is the online “Building a Points Engine” session, and is it recorded?

It will be held online before the workshop. The date and time will be announced soon. We will not be able to provide a recording, so try to attend live.

What is included in the registration fee?

A 90-minute online session, Building a Points Engine, before the workshop, and the full-day, in-person workshop from 10 AM to 5 PM, with lunch and tea/coffee through the day. You also take home workshop resources, reference guides and checklists, and get a complimentary 30-minute one-on-one online consultation to use within 3 months of the workshop.

Can I bring my spouse or partner?

Yes, you can. Each attendee needs their own seat, but we offer 50% discount on any additional seats booked by the same participant for their spouses or partners. Since many families pool their points, this one comes up a lot. Please contact us about how to book the discounted seats.

Should I bring a laptop?

It is optional. A laptop can be handy if you want to follow along as we search for award flights and hotels. But a phone or a tablet works fine as well, and you can simply take notes.

Where exactly is the venue, and how big is the group?

The workshop is near Delhi-Gurgaon border (NH8). We share the exact venue with registered participants. Seats are limited, so the group stays small enough for real questions and conversation.

Can I cancel or transfer my seat?

The fee is non-refundable. If you cannot make it, you can transfer your seat to someone else.
